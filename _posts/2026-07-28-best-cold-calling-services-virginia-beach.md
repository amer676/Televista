## Step-by-Step Implementation

Getting your cold calling operation off the ground in Virginia Beach doesn't require a 47-step checklist. But you do need to do it in the right order — skip a step and you'll be fixing problems mid-campaign when it's too expensive to fix them.

**Step 1: Pull and scrub your list first.** Before a single call gets made, build your target list through [BatchLeads](https://www.batchleads.io) or [PropStream](https://www.propstream.com). Focus on absentee owners, tax-delinquent, and probate leads in zip codes like 23452, 23462, and 23455. A clean list is doing 40% of the work before anyone picks up the phone.

**Step 2: Sort out your compliance posture.** Virginia wholesalers operate under a specific legal framework — under [Virginia Administrative Code 23VAC10-500-350](https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title23/agency10/chapter500/section350), wholesale trade is defined as selling to buyers who will then resell, not directly to end consumers for personal use. That distinction matters for your BPOL tax classification and how you're structuring deals. Know where you sit before you scale.

**Step 3: Pick a service that already has the infrastructure.** Plenty of wholesalers waste two weeks shopping for a dialer, then another week getting numbers, then another setting up integrations. Look for a cold calling company that already comes with a power dialer and skip-traced numbers — as users in the [Real Estate Agent Referral Network](https://www.facebook.com/groups/therealestateagentreferralnetwork/posts/1735375263997217) have flagged, this is one of the most common friction points when hiring outbound help. Bonus if they already have access to tools like [Vulcan7](https://www.vulcan7.com) or [RedX](https://www.theredx.com) — you don't want to be paying for those separately on top of service fees.

> **Pro tip:** Ask any service you're considering: "Do you provide the dialer, or do I need to?" If they can't answer that in one sentence, keep looking.

**Step 4: Define your handoff process before calls start.** Agreed-upon criteria for what counts as a qualified lead, how it gets logged in [REsimpli](https://www.resimpli.com) or whatever CRM you're running, and who follows up — nail this down beforehand. Most pipeline leaks happen right here.

**Step 5: Run a test batch, then review.** Don't commit to a full month before you've seen how the callers handle objections. Listen to recordings. Adjust the script. Tighten the list criteria if you're getting too many disconnected numbers.

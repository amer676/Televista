Austin's market right now — [6.7 months of inventory](https://munozaustin.com/blogs/will-the-austin-real-estate-market-be-better-for-sellers-in-2026/), homes sitting unsold for nearly two months — actually rewards wholesalers who can get to motivated sellers *before* those sellers realize they're motivated. ## Step-by-Step Implementation

Pull your list first. Everything downstream depends on it.

Run a filtered search in [BatchLeads](https://www.batchleads.io) targeting Austin zip codes — focus on absentee owners, high equity, and properties with extended days on market. Given that homes are averaging [56–57 days on market](https://munozaustin.com/blogs/will-the-austin-real-estate-market-be-better-for-sellers-in-2026/) right now, that last filter alone can surface a lot of motivated seller candidates. Skip trace immediately, before anyone else does.

> **Pro tip:** Don't build a 10,000-person list and then wonder why your callers sound burned out by noon. Tighter lists, better conversations. 500 genuinely warm records beats 2,000 cold ones every time.

Once your list is clean, load it into [Mojo Dialer](https://www.mojosells.com) or [CallTools](https://www.calltools.com) — both handle Austin-area compliance reasonably well, and both let you build calling queues by neighborhood. Run by neighborhood if you can. Callers can reference local context ("I've been seeing a lot of activity around the 78745 zip lately") and it doesn't feel like a robo-blast.

**Script your opener around the market reality, not a generic pitch.** With [57% of Austin listings taking price cuts](https://munozaustin.com/blogs/will-the-austin-real-estate-market-be-better-for-sellers-in-2026/) averaging 9% reductions, a lot of sellers already know they're in a tough spot. You don't need to manufacture urgency — acknowledge it, offer an alternative.

From there, the workflow looks like this:

1. **Dial** — target 150–200 contacts per caller per day, depending on connect rate
2. **Qualify** — timeline, motivation, condition, rough equity position
3. **CRM entry** — log everything in [REsimpli](https://www.resimpli.com) or [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com) immediately, not at end of day
4. **Follow-up sequence** — set callbacks for anyone who wasn't ready but wasn't a hard no
5. **Appointment handoff** — warm transfer or scheduled callback to your acquisition team

Managing that whole loop in-house is genuinely hard to do consistently. That's where outsourcing to a team like [Televista](/services.html) makes sense — trained callers, managed follow-up cadences, CRM integration already built out. Long story short — less for you to break.

## Step-by-Step Implementation

Getting a cold calling operation off the ground in Atlanta isn't complicated — but sequencing it wrong will waste weeks and money.

**Step 1: Build your list before you hire anyone.**

Pull targeted lists from [BatchLeads](https://batchleads.io) or [PropStream](https://propstream.com) first. Filter by equity, absentee owner status, and pre-foreclosure flags across high-activity zip codes — think Fulton, DeKalb, Clayton. A caller with a bad list is just burning your dial hours. Don't skip this.

**Step 2: Lock in your CRM and tracking setup.**

You need somewhere for lead data to land before the first call goes out, not after. [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com) starts at [$25/month per user](https://slashdot.org/software/real-estate-crm/for-call-tracking/) with solid call-tracking capability if you're scaling past one caller. REsimpli works fine too if you want something built for wholesalers.

> **Pro tip:** Map your disposition stages in the CRM before calls start — "Callback", "Hot Lead", "Not Interested", "Wrong Number". You'll thank yourself later when the data actually tells you something useful instead of sitting in a spreadsheet nobody reads.

**Step 3: Nail the compliance layer.**

Georgia has its own regulations around outbound marketing calls, and consent can be revoked at any time — worth reviewing [how consent and opt-outs work in practice](https://www.justanswer.com/business-law/ezbtb-georgia-long-list-homeowner-data.html) before you scale. Scrub your lists against the DNC registry. Not optional.

**Step 4: Write or source a script built for motivated sellers.**

Generic scripts get hung up on. Your script needs to address why you're calling, what you're offering, and how to handle "I'm not interested" without folding immediately. Test two or three script variations before settling on one — I'd honestly recommend this even if your first script feels solid.

**Step 5: Hire, train, or outsource — and monitor closely.**

Whether you're using a freelancer from [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/services/product/admin-customer-support-real-estate-wholesale-cold-calling-for-agents-and-investors-1583202665728663552), building an in-house team, or going with a full-service operation like [Televista](/services.html), the setup doesn't run itself. Listen to calls weekly. Track connect rate, conversation rate, and appointments set separately — treating them as one number is where most teams lose visibility.

Week one should be small. Test, adjust, then scale.

Long story short — you get what you pay for, and in Atlanta's market, you'll feel the difference fast.

- **Assuming one script fits every zip code.** Sellers in Fulton County respond differently than absentee owners in Clayton. Adapt your approach.

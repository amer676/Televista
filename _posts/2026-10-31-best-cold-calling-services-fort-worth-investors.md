## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Most Fort Worth investors don't fail at cold calling because they picked the wrong dialer. They fail because they skip the boring fundamentals — and the market punishes that fast.

**Mistake 1: Ignoring Texas compliance.** [Texas is a "Mini-TCPA State" with a "High Risk" compliance rating](https://tcpaguide.org/states/texas/solar-sales), statute amended again in 2025. Calling on Sunday before noon? That's a violation. Allowed windows are **9:00 AM–9:00 PM weekdays and Saturday**, and **noon–9:00 PM on Sunday**. Most outsourced services know this. A lot of DIY operations don't — until they get a complaint.

**Mistake 2: Skipping list quality.** I've seen investors spin up dialers and immediately start burning through bad data — no skip tracing, outdated numbers, wrong ownership records. [BatchLeads](https://batchleads.io) and [PropStream](https://www.propstream.com) exist for a reason. Don't cheap out here.

**Mistake 3: Chasing lead volume without qualification standards.** Some services promise big numbers — [Call Savvys, for example, guarantees 20 to 40 exclusive motivated-seller leads per caller per month](https://www.callsavvys.com/comparisons/best-cold-calling-companies-for-real-estate-investors). Sounds great. But a lead count means nothing if your callers aren't filtering for actual motivation. Define what a "qualified lead" means before anyone picks up the phone.

> **Pro tip:** Set your disposition criteria in [REsimpli](https://resimpli.com) before your campaign launches — not after. Cleaning up messy lead data mid-campaign is a nightmare you don't want.

**Mistake 4: No follow-up system.** Cold calling without a structured follow-up sequence is just leaving money on the table. Most motivated sellers won't say yes on the first call. Build the drip before you dial.

**Step 1: Build the right list first.**

Pull absentee owners, pre-foreclosures, and out-of-state landlords through [BatchLeads](https://www.batchleads.io) or [PropStream](https://www.propstream.com). Filter by zip code — 79902, 79912, 79936 — and cross-reference ownership dates. Long-hold absentees are your warmest segment in this market.

**Step 2: Scrub for DNC compliance before a single dial.**

Non-negotiable. El Paso has a high military-household concentration, and those numbers rotate constantly. Skip tracing through [BatchLeads](https://www.batchleads.io) pulls updated contact info, but you still need a DNC scrub layer before loading into your dialer.

**Step 3: Load into your dialer and set up call dispositions.**

[Mojo Dialer](https://www.mojosells.com) is solid for high-volume work. Set at least five dispositions — callback, not interested, no answer, wrong number, hot lead — so your follow-up pipeline doesn't turn into a pile of sticky notes nobody checks.

> **Pro tip:** Don't build a massive list and blast through it once. A tighter list dialed three times over two weeks consistently outperforms a giant list hit once and abandoned. I've gone back and forth on this one, but the data keeps pointing the same direction.

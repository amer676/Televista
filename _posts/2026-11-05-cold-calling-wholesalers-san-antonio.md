The compliance picture clarified a bit with the [Texas telemarketing law update](https://www.losey.law/texas-telemarketing-law-update-certain-consent-based-sms-exempt/) (last modified May 2026), which confirmed that consent-based text marketing businesses don't have to register as telemarketers under [Chapter 302 of the Texas Business and Commerce Code](https://www.losey.law/texas-telemarketing-law-update-certain-consent-based-sms-exempt/) — and they're exempt from that old **$10,000 security deposit** requirement. Good news if you're running SMS alongside your calls. But live outbound dialing still falls under [FCC telemarketing rules](https://www.fcc.gov/general/telemarketing) and the [National Do Not Call Registry](https://www.facebook.com/LeavenworthProsecutor/posts/-want-fewer-unwanted-sales-callsthe-national-do-not-call-registry-helps-reduce-u/1434703032179431/) — no shortcuts there.

> **Pro tip:** Don't conflate the SMS exemption with live call compliance. Two different buckets. I've seen wholesalers get burned assuming one covers the other.

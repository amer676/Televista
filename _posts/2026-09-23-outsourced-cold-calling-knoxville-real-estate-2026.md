## Common Mistakes to Avoid

Most investors don't fail at outsourced cold calling because the channel doesn't work. They fail because they skip the boring stuff — compliance, list hygiene, feedback loops.

**Mistake #1: Ignoring the regulatory side.** [NAR](https://www.nar.realtor/) has been clear since at least 2017 that cold calling and telemarketing are heavily regulated — TCPA, Do Not Call registry, state-level rules on top of that. If your outsourced caller isn't scrubbing against the DNC list before every campaign, you're exposed. Full stop. I've seen investors hand a raw BatchLeads export to a calling team with zero scrubbing. That's not a strategy, that's a liability.

> **Pro tip:** Ask any outsourced team you're considering how they handle DNC compliance before you sign anything. If they fumble the answer, walk away.

**Mistake #2: Handing off the process and disappearing.** Outsourcing doesn't mean forgetting. Your caller needs feedback — which leads are converting in [REsimpli](https://www.resimpli.com), which neighborhoods are producing garbage, what objections are coming up. No feedback means no iteration. Dead campaigns fast.

**Mistake #3: Treating all lists the same.** A cold absentee owner list and a tax-delinquent pre-foreclosure list aren't the same conversation. Different pain points, different scripts, different cadence.

**Mistake #4: Optimizing for call volume, not appointments.** Dials are vanity. Appointments are what matter for real estate [cold calling ROI](https://televistaleadgeneration.com/blog/2026/07/29/cold-calling-roi-richmond-va-real-estate/).

And honestly — **mistake #5** is the one nobody talks about: hiring the cheapest option and expecting top-tier Knoxville real estate investor leads. You get what you fund. If the outsourced cold calling services you're reviewing for Knoxville real estate investors are competing purely on price, that's worth scrutinizing before you commit.

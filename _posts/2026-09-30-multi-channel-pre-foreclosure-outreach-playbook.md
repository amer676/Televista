## Step-by-Step Implementation

Pull your list first. [BatchLeads](https://batchleads.io) or [PropStream](https://www.propstream.com) — either works. Filter for stacked indicators: pre-foreclosure notice **plus** tax delinquency, or vacancy **plus** probate filing. Single signals get you a name. Stacked signals get you a motivated seller. [ClosersLeague](https://closersleague.com/blog/seller-motivation-identification-guide/) confirmed this directly — verifiable constraints like these predict below-market deals better than stated interest alone.

Set a calendar reminder for monthly list refreshes. Not quarterly. Monthly. Records older than **60 to 90 days** lose accuracy as defaults cure and violations resolve — [ClosersLeague](https://closersleague.com/blog/seller-motivation-identification-guide/) is explicit about this — and stale data is just expensive busywork.

Once you've got a clean, fresh list, skip trace it through BatchLeads or a dedicated service like [Skip Genie](https://www.skipgenie.com). You want phone, email, and mailing address on every contact. Don't skip this step thinking you'll "just cold call." Multi-channel is the point.

> **Pro tip:** Run your skip-traced numbers through a DNC scrub before you dial anything. Seriously. Compliance isn't optional, and the regulatory landscape around outreach has tightened since the CFPB compliance requirements came into effect — documented as far back as [February 2018](https://downloads.regulations.gov/CFPB-2019-0016-0002/content.pdf).

1. **Direct mail drops first** — gets you in the house before the call

4. **Email day 10-12** — AI nurturing sequences can handle this drip automatically

Load your dials into [Mojo Dialer](https://www.mojosells.com) or [CallTools](https://www.calltools.com). Power dialing pre-foreclosure lists eats time if you're doing it manually — don't.

Chasing single signals is another one I see constantly. One missed payment doesn't make a motivated seller. [ClosersLeague](https://closersleague.com/blog/seller-motivation-identification-guide/) confirms what most experienced investors already suspect: **stacked distress indicators** (pre-foreclosure notice plus tax delinquency, vacancy plus probate) predict closed, below-market deals better than any single trigger on its own. Compliance doesn't get easier by ignoring it. Check your DNC scrubs. Every time.

And honestly? Most people skip the skip tracing quality check entirely. Bad numbers mean your dialer's burning time on disconnected lines instead of actual owners.

## Step-by-Step Implementation

Most people overthink the setup and never actually start. Don't do that.

**Step 1: Build your list before you dial a single number.** Pull absentee homeowner lists and pre-foreclosure leads out of [BatchLeads](https://batchleads.io) or [PropStream](https://www.propstream.com) — filter by equity percentage, out-of-state owner address, and any tax delinquency flags you can find. **Step 2: Get compliant first, not later.** Florida requires telemarketer registration for 2026, and skipping it is an expensive mistake. Per [TCPA Guide](https://tcpaguide.org/states/florida/registration), the business registration application fee runs **$500**, individual registration is **$300**, and annual renewal is another **$500** every December 31st. Scrub your lists against the Do Not Call registry before your first dial. Non-negotiable.

**Step 3: Load your leads into a power dialer.** [Mojo Dialer](https://www.mojosells.com) or [CallTools](https://www.calltools.com) — either works. The point is you're not manually dialing. Set your call windows between 9am–12pm and 4pm–7pm local time when connect rates are highest.

**Step 4: Work a real script.** Not a 4-page monster — just a tight opener, a pain-point question, and a clear ask for a conversation. Keep it conversational (people hang up on robots instantly).

**Step 5: Follow up inside [REsimpli](https://resimpli.com).** Most motivated sellers Cape Coral investors close on weren't ready to sell on the first call. Track every touchpoint, set callbacks, and don't let a warm lead go cold because you forgot to follow up.
